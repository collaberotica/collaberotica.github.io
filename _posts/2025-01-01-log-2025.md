## December 10

**Introducing: Collections**

Collections are a way for you to organize stories and chapters you like, and share them with the world. Everyone can now create collections and add stories/chapters to them.

This is a new top-level feature! On the "Collections" page, you can see the collections people have created:

<img src="/assets/img/collectindex.png" style="border:2px solid black; border-radius:15px;margin:auto;display: block; margin: auto;"/>

While reading a story/chapter, use the new "Collect" button on the navbar to add it to a collection:

<img src="/assets/img/colecbutdesk.png" style="border:2px solid black; border-radius:15px;margin:auto;display: block; margin: auto;"/>
<p style="text-align: center;">
	(desktop)
</p>

<img src="/assets/img/colecbutmob.png" style="border:2px solid black; border-radius:15px;margin:auto;display: block; margin: auto;"/>
<p style="text-align: center;">
	(mobile)
</p>

From here, you can add it to any collection of yours.

<img src="/assets/img/colecwork.png" style="border:2px solid black; border-radius:15px;margin:auto;display: block; margin: auto;"/>

Of course, you can also create a collection. You can create a public collection, which other people can see, or you can create a private collection:

<img src="/assets/img/colecworknewcolec.png" style="border:2px solid black; border-radius:15px;margin:auto;display: block; margin: auto;"/>

A couple empty collections are already created for you, for ease of use.

To see your private collections, go to your user page:

<img src="/assets/img/colecyours.png" style="border:2px solid black; border-radius:15px;margin:auto;display: block; margin: auto;"/>

This is the first time that readers will really able to promote and share works within CollabErotica, so I'm excited to see how people use collections.

Some ideas:

- A collection of works that you think deserve more recognition
- A private collection of works you want to read, but haven't read yet
- A collection of works that really scratch your particular itch ;)

By the way, this is an "MVP" or "first draft" of the collections feature. There are a couple things I want to add, including a way to see which collections a story/chapter has been added to. Also, for now, only public works can be added to collections.

As always, you can leave feedback [here](https://collaberotica.org/tos/contact).

## October 13

- Paths have been upgraded. For interactives, you can now easily read the whole story up to any chapter.

From any interactive chapter, if you select the Path button, this new UI will appear:

<img src="/assets/img/path1.png" style="border:2px solid black; border-radius:15px;margin:auto;display: block; margin: auto;"/>

This will take you back to chapter 1, with the choice leading to the original chapter highlighted.

<img src="/assets/img/path2.png" style="border:2px solid black; border-radius:15px;margin:auto;display: block; margin: auto;"/>

As you continue reading, every choice leading up to the original chapter will be highlighted.

The highlights also appear on the Outline:

<img src="/assets/img/path3.png" style="border:2px solid black; border-radius:15px;margin:auto;display: block; margin: auto;"/>

You can also start the path up to 10 chapters before the selected chapter.
