## February 24

- You will now receive emails when someone adds a chapter to your content. Specifically, when:
  - A chapter is added to your story, or a story you edit
  - A chapter is added after any chapter you wrote
- These emails are opt-out. You can adjust your chapter email preferences here: [https://collaberotica.org/users/edit](https://collaberotica.org/users/edit)
- We would like to know what other kinds of emails you might like to receive. Let us know here or at: [https://collaberotica.org/tos/feedack](https://collaberotica.org/tos/feedack)
- Adjusted how views are counted. They should be more accurate now.
- Made the age gate/cookie consent banner a bit friendlier
- Added a secret

As always, feedback can be left [here](https://collaberotica.org/tos/feedack)
