## December 31, 2024

- Dislikes have been removed. After more than a year of Collaberotica, we have seen:
  - Approximately 3000 likes across stories, chapters, users, and series.
  - 15 dislikes.

  It is not a popular feature, and in our opinion, it is not a productive way of leaving feedback for authors, nor does it help people filter out content they might not like.

As always, feedback can be left [here](https://collaberotica.org/tos/feedack)
Or on [discord](https://collaberotica.org/discord)
