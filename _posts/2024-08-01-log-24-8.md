## August 29, 2024

- When adding or editing tags, you can now copy and paste a list of tags separated by commas to add them all at once
  - For example, copying and pasting  `aa,bb,cc,dd,ee,ff,gg,hh,ii,jj` into the tags field will add those ten tags
- Memory leaks that had been causing the site to briefly go offline every few days have been fixed
