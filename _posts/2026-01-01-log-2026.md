## February 24

Redesigned the Dashboard page.

- It's easier to see all the content and activity you've created. Stories, chapters, likes, comments, etc.
- You can filter your likes and follows by stories, chapters, etc.
- It's easier to see all stories shared with you. For example, if you can see a private story, or if you edit a story.
- It's easier to see whether you have any chapters to review.
- And more!
