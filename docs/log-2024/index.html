2024 Changelog

Jan 01, 2024 • Collaberotica Jan 01, 2024 Collaberotica
Tags: devlog

December 31, 2024

  • Dislikes have been removed. Thus far, we have seen:
    • Over 3000 likes across stories, chapters, users, and series
    • 15 dislikes

    It is not a popular feature, and in our opinion, it is not a productive way of leaving feedback for authors, nor does it help people filter out content they might not like.

As always, feedback can be left here Or on discord

August 29, 2024

  • When adding or editing tags, you can now copy and paste a list of tags separated by commas to add them all at once
    • For example, copying and pasting aa,bb,cc,dd,ee,ff,gg,hh,ii,jj into the tags field will add those ten tags
  • Memory leaks that had been causing the site to briefly go offline every few days have been fixed

As always, feedback can be left here Or on discord

February 2, 2024

New Changes ✨

  • Big Browse page revamp!
    • Now called “Read page”
    • Now a feed style with two feeds: things you follow, and all recent items
    • Stats, story popularity, rankings, etc. have been moved to separate pages
    • The page should load faster
  • The Following page became redundant and was removed in favor of the Following feed
  • Certain types of notifications became redundant and were removed in favor of the Following feed
  • On your User Profile page, it is now clear whether your likes/follows are private
  • “The End” no longer appears on ending chapters automatically. A story’s final words should be left to the author
  • Page titles are less big
  • Fixed some UI issues on Safari
  • UI improvements to Polls, Chapters Stories, Search Results, and general consistency

As always, feedback can be left here Or on discord
